# Code of Conduct

AI Usage follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in this project.
- Don't share other people's private information, including logins, tokens or conversation content.

If something feels wrong, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/seanwoo-personal/ai-usage/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@seanwoo-personal](https://github.com/seanwoo-personal). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove content or block participants who don't follow these guidelines.
